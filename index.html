The Angel of Dawn

Long ago, the sun refused to rise, and the world fell into endless night. Every star grew dim, and every heart grew afraid.

High above the clouds, one angel named Seraphine awoke. She carried a spark of golden light in her hands and flew toward the darkness.

Seraphine opened her wings, and her light spread across the horizon. The sun rose at last, and the first dawn began.

Every morning since is her gift to the world. The End.
